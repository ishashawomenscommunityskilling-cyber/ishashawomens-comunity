What we do

Programs

Practical, community-rooted initiatives that build skills, income, and resilience for women and girls in Ishasha.

Agricultural production

Our community enterprise produces bananas, tomatoes, rice, groundnuts, maize, watermelons, vegetables, pumpkins, poultry, goats, and coffee to generate income and practical skills for local women.

Agro-tourism and cultural experiences

Visitors can enjoy traditional food, banana juice and coffee experiences, village walks, and traditional dance and drama performances in the Ishasha area near Queen Elizabeth National Park.

Women's small business empowerment

A flagship initiative provides $100 in seed capital per woman to support small business start-up, household income growth, and long-term self-reliance.

Village Savings and Loans Associations

Low-interest community lending and savings schemes help families build resilience, grow enterprise, and strengthen financial independence.

Education and child welfare

We support vulnerable children, with special attention to the girl child, alongside wider advocacy for child and maternal health, safe water, hygiene, and sanitation.

Environmental conservation

Working with conservation partners near Queen Elizabeth National Park, we promote sustainable resource use and local awareness around environmental protection.
