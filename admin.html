Manage homepage slides

Upload a photo, add the person’s name and a short description. New slides appear automatically in the homepage hero slider. Photos are stored on Cloudinary; slide details are saved in this browser.

Cloudinary setup (required once)

1. Create a free account at cloudinary.com
2. Settings → Upload → Add upload preset → choose Unsigned
3. Paste your Cloud Name and Upload Preset below, then click Save.
