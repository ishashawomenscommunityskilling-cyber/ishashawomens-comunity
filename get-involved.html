Get Involved

Whether you are an individual supporter, organisation, or visitor interested in agro-tourism and cultural experiences, there are many ways to walk with the Ishasha community.

Donate

Fund seed capital ($100 per woman), farm inputs, education materials, or the greenhouse project. Every contribution strengthens household income and community resilience.

See how to donate →

Partner & collaborate

We welcome partnerships with local, national, and international organisations on skills training, safe water, conservation, education, and livelihoods.

Contact the team →

Visit & experience

Explore agro-tourism and cultural experiences: traditional food, banana juice and coffee, village walks, dance and drama near Queen Elizabeth National Park.

Plan a visit →

Advocate

Help amplify the community’s call for safe water, girl-child education, and sustainable livelihoods. Share the story of Ishasha women and girls.
