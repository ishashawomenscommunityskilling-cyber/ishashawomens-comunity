Community-rooted women empowerment in Ishasha, Uganda

Empowering Women for a Greater Community

Women And Girls Empowered Communities Transformed

IWSDP is a community-based, not-for-profit organisation in Ishasha, Kanungu District, Uganda, supporting women and girls through skills development, livelihoods, education, conservation, and community welfare.

Located on the doorstep of Queen Elizabeth National Park, IWSDP exists to provide education, skills training, conservation awareness, and socio-economic empowerment for women and girls in the Ishasha community.

Vision and mission

A stronger future shaped by women and girls

Vision

Empowering women for a greater community.

Mission

To provide education through skills development, conservation of the environment, and socio-economic welfare for the women of our community in order to improve their standards of living — empowering them to feel confident and supported as they navigate difficult circumstances.

Our values

Respect Compassion Innovation Equality Honesty Integrity Transparency Confidentiality

What we focus on

  • Skills training and capacity building for women
  • Small business empowerment through agriculture, livestock, and agro-tourism
  • Environmental conservation near Queen Elizabeth National Park
  • Village Savings and Loans Associations (VSLA) and microfinance
  • Support for vulnerable children, especially the girl child, in education
  • Cultural tourism through traditional dance, drama, and village walk experiences
