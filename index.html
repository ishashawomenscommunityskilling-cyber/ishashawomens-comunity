Vision and mission

A stronger future shaped by women and girls

Vision

Empowering women for a greater community.

Mission

To provide education through skills development, conservation of the environment, and socio-economic welfare for the women of our community.

RespectCompassionInnovation EqualityHonestyIntegrity TransparencyConfidentiality

What we focus on

  • Skills training and capacity building for women
  • Small business empowerment through agriculture and agro-tourism
  • Environmental conservation near Queen Elizabeth National Park
  • Village Savings and Loans Associations
  • Support for vulnerable children, especially the girl child
  • Cultural tourism through dance, drama and village walks
