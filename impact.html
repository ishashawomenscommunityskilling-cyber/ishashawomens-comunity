Focus areas that drive impact

  • Skills training and capacity building for women
  • Small business empowerment through agriculture, livestock, and agro-tourism
  • Environmental conservation near Queen Elizabeth National Park
  • Village Savings and Loans Associations (VSLA) and microfinance
  • Support for vulnerable children, especially the girl child
  • Cultural tourism and community livelihood experiences
